🏔️ Expedition Chronicle: The Subalpine Realm of Formosa

Few international mountaineers realize that Taiwan is one of the world's most mountainous islands. In a land area roughly the size of Belgium or Maryland, Taiwan packs an astonishing 268 peaks towering above 3,000 meters (9,843 ft). This rugged vertebral column—anchored by the sacred Central Mountain Range (中央山脈) and the Yushan Range—acts as the island's protective shield, breaking Pacific storms and preserving ancient biological ecosystems.

Here, two alpine journeys stand supreme: Yushan Main Peak (玉山主峰, 3,952m / 12,966 ft), the highest mountain in Northeast Asia—standing taller than Japan’s Mount Fuji—and Hehuanshan (合歡山, 3,417m), an accessible, awe-inspiring introduction to Taiwan’s famed "100 Peaks" (百岳, Baiyue). Whether you are securing a coveted bed at Paiyun Lodge or pitching a tent under crystalline alpine galaxies, this comprehensive expedition manual equips you for an unforgettable high-altitude adventure.

⚠️ High-Altitude Safety Notice (AMS & Microclimates): At 3,000m to 3,952m, atmospheric pressure and oxygen density drop by roughly 35% compared to sea level. Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) can affect anyone regardless of fitness. Never ascend directly from sea level to the Yushan summit in a single day. Spend a mandatory acclimatization night at Tataka (2,600m), Alishan (2,200m), or Cingjing (1,800m) before pushing into subalpine terrain. Carry Acetazolamide (Diamox) under medical consultation, and descend immediately if severe headaches or nausea arise.

🌡️ Four-Season Mountain Weather: When to Summit & What to Expect

Spring (Spring Awakening & Blooms) March – May 0°C to 12°C
(Night: -2°C to 4°C)		 Yushan and Hehuanshan erupt in wild rhododendron and alpine azalea blooms across rocky crags. Stable weather early, afternoon mists frequent. Waterproof hard-shell jacket, thermal base layers, UV sunglasses. Late-season cold snaps can still produce sudden freezing rain.
Summer (Typhoon & Afternoon Storms) June – August 6°C to 16°C
(Night: 4°C to 8°C)		 Pleasant midday temperatures. However, convective afternoon thunderstorms (13:00–16:00) occur daily. Potential summer typhoons can close trails. Start hiking at 04:00 AM to summit before midday rain. 100% waterproof dry bags, pack covers, high-SPF sunscreen, and electrolytes.

(Night: -4°C to 3°C)		 The undisputed peak season: dry, cloudless cobalt skies, breathtaking seas of clouds (雲海), and crisp visibility extending across the Taiwan Strait. Windproof soft-shell, warm fleece, down jacket (800+ fill power) for summit ridge winds, headlamp with extra lithium batteries.
Winter (Frost, Ice & Snow Cap) December – February -8°C to 5°C
(Night: -12°C to -2°C)		 Subzero arctic conditions. Hehuanshan turns into a snow playground; Yushan Main Peak enters its restricted "Winter Snow Season" (雪季管制). Strict mountaineering ice gear mandatory: Certified 10+ point crampons, mountaineering ice axe, climbing helmet, thermal gloves, snow goggles.

📑 The Yushan Permit Blueprint: How Foreigners Beat the Lottery

4-Month Advantage ｜ Foreign Quota

1. The Foreign Advance Quota Rule

Paiyun Lodge (排雲山莊) ｜ Sunday to Thursday Nights

The Yushan National Park grants 24 reserved slots per day specifically for foreign passport holders staying at Paiyun Lodge on non-holiday Sunday-to-Thursday nights. Applications open exactly 4 months (120 days) prior to your hike date at 07:00 AM Taiwan Time. Apply on the minute—slots vanish within seconds!

🎒 Essential Alpine Packing Checklist (Yushan & Hehuan)

🧥 Three-Layer Clothing System
  • Base Layer: 100% Merino wool long-sleeve top & bottoms (odor-resistant, insulates when wet).
  • Mid Layer: Technical fleece or lightweight grid-fleece hoodie.
  • Insulation: 800+ fill power down jacket with hood (essential for summit ridge windchill).
  • Outer Shell: Gore-Tex / 3-layer breathable waterproof hard-shell jacket & rain pants (strictly no cheap ponchos).
  • Accessories: Merino beanie, UV neck gaiter, fleece gloves + waterproof windproof over-mittens.
🥾 Footwear & Trail Hardware
  • Boots: Broken-in mid-to-high-cut waterproof hiking boots with Vibram soles for shale stability.
  • Socks: 2 pairs of heavy-cushion Merino wool hiking socks.
  • Trekking Poles: Pair of sturdy locking poles (crucial for knee joints during the 1,350m descent).
  • Headlamp: Minimum 300 lumens with spare lithium batteries (standard alkaline fails in freezing temps).
  • Backpack: 30L–40L technical pack with integrated rain cover.
💊 Medical, Nutrition & Survival
  • Altitude Medication: Acetazolamide (Diamox), Ibuprofen (for headaches), blister tape/moleskin.
  • Hydration: 2L capacity (insulated thermos for hot water + wide-mouth Nalgene bottle).
  • Trail Fuel: 3,000+ calories of energy bars, jerky, electrolyte tablets, and high-carb snack packs.
  • Documents: Physical printed copy of Yushan National Park permit + original Passport.
  • Emergency: Whistle, thermal emergency bivvy blanket, offline GPS maps (Gaia GPS / GPX loaded).
