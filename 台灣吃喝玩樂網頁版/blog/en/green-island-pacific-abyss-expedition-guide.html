🌊 Historical & Geothermal Chronicle: The Isolation of Fire and Tides

Emerging like an emerald jewel 33 kilometers off the southeastern coast of Taitung, Green Island (originally known as Sanasai or Bonso No Tona in indigenous Yami and Amis oral traditions) was forged from an extinct volcanic arc. Surrounded by oceanic trenches exceeding 1,000 meters in depth, the island is washed by the nutrient-rich, ultra-clear Kuroshio Current (黑潮), creating crystal visibilities frequently exceeding 30 to 40 meters.

The island’s isolation has borne two profoundly contrasting identities: in the mid-20th century, it was the secluded site of the Oasis Villa political prison during Taiwan's White Terror era, holding intellectuals and democracy activists. Today, that sobering history at the National Human Rights Museum stands alongside one of the world's only three open-air saltwater geothermal springs (Zhaori Hot Springs) and an elite international scuba diving sanctuary.

The Big Mushroom (大香菇) Shilang Reef. A massive, single Porites coral head standing 12 meters tall, estimated to be over 1,000 years old. 16m–19m.
Visibility: 30m+.
Open Water friendly.		 Shilang Shore Dive Pass
Shark Point (滾水坪 - Hammerhead Reef) Deep ocean trench off the southern tip. Schooling scalloped hammerhead sharks gathering between January and March. 30m–45m.
Advanced Open Water + drift diving experience strictly required.		 Pelagic Boat Charter
Zhaori Saltwater Hot Springs (朝日溫泉) One of only three saltwater geothermal springs in the world. Natural circular rock baths right on the tidal shelf. Sea level tidal pools.
Ideal for midnight stargazing or sunrise soaking at 5 AM.		 Zhaori Priority Ticket
