## How to Choose a Taipei Cafe

### By Coffee Style

- **Pour-over:** Best for visitors who want to taste single-origin beans and brewing detail.
- **Espresso-based:** Suitable for latte, flat white, cappuccino or milk coffee lovers.
- **Taiwanese-origin coffee:** Good for travelers who want to taste local-grown beans.
- **Dessert pairing:** Choose a cafe known for cakes, puddings, cinnamon rolls or baked goods.

### By Space Type

- **Old-house cafe:** Character, wood, brick or terrazzo details; seats may be limited.
- **Roaster / specialty cafe:** Focus on beans, brewing method and coffee education.
- **Work-friendly cafe:** Wi-Fi, sockets and longer-stay tolerance; check minimum spend.
- **Dessert cafe:** Strong sweet menu; coffee may be secondary.

### Before You Sit Down

- Is there a minimum spend or time limit?
- Are laptops and long stays allowed?
- Is Wi-Fi available, and do sockets work?
- Does the cafe serve dessert or light meals?
- Are weekends busy enough to require waiting?

## How to Order Coffee in Taiwan

1. **Espresso-based drinks:** Latte, cappuccino, Americano and flat white are commonly understood.
2. **Milk options:** Fresh milk is standard; oat or other plant milk may be limited and sometimes costs extra.
3. **Sweetness:** Most espresso drinks are not pre-sweetened. Ask for syrup separately if you prefer sweet coffee.
4. **Pour-over:** Ask what single-origin beans are available and whether the shop can describe acidity, body and flavor notes.
5. **Dessert pairing:** Cinnamon roll, pudding, honey cake or crêpe often pairs better with milk coffee than a light pour-over.
