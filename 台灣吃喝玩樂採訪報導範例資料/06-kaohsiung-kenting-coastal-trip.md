### Kaohsiung Food Types to Recognize

- **Seafood:** Oysters, squid, fish and shellfish; ask about freshness and market price before ordering whole fish.
- **Papaya milk and fruit drinks:** Common sweet drinks; sweetness can often be adjusted.
- **Local rice and noodle dishes:** Braised pork rice, beef noodle soup, rice porridge and dumplings.
- **Night-market fried snacks:** Chicken cutlet, salty crispy chicken, sausage, fried tofu and grilled squid.

### Coastal Road Notes

- Do not drive or ride after dark unless you are familiar with the route.
- Coastal roads can be windy; secure belongings and check weather alerts.
- Summer brings strong sun and possible afternoon thunderstorms; bring sunscreen, water and a hat.
- During typhoon season, beaches and water activities may close for safety.

## Practical Notes

1. **Book water activities through licensed operators.** Check insurance, equipment, weather cancellation and safety instructions.
2. **Seafood prices vary.** Ask whether seafood is priced by portion, weight or market price before ordering.
3. **Sun and wind are strong.** Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and light wind protection.
4. **Check last transport.** Evening buses from Kenting back to Kaohsiung can be limited.
5. **Respect coastal safety.** Do not climb unstable rocks or enter closed beaches.
