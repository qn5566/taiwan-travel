### City Bus

Buses cover neighborhoods not reached by MRT. Check the route number, destination display and stop location. Bus announcements and displays may be in Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka and English; verify your stop on a map app.

### YouBike and Walking

YouBike is useful for short riverside or neighborhood rides. Check bike availability, parking rules, helmet rules and whether your card or app can unlock a bike. Return bikes to designated docking stations.

### Taxi

Taxis are useful for late nights, heavy luggage or cross-district trips. Use flagged taxis, official taxi stands or licensed ride apps. Confirm the destination and fare estimate before departure.

## Intercity Transport

### High-Speed Rail (HSR)

**Best for:** Fast trips between Taipei, Taichung, Tainan and Kaohsiung.  
**Notes:** HSR stations are often outside the old city center; add local train, bus or taxi time to your plan. Book early for popular dates and weekends.

### Taiwan Railway (TRA)

**Best for:** Smaller towns, coastal routes, mountain-line towns and areas not served by HSR.  
**Notes:** Local trains may be slower but stop at more stations. Check whether your train is local, express or limited-stop.

### Intercity Bus

**Best for:** Budget travel, towns without rail access and flexible schedules.  
**Notes:** Bus terminals vary by city; luggage space and schedule reliability should be checked before a tight connection.

### Rental Car

**Best for:** Coastal routes, rural towns, mountain areas and groups with luggage.  
**Notes:** Read local road rules, parking restrictions, insurance and license requirements. Automatic-transmission vehicles may be limited in some areas.

### Scooter Rental

Scooters are common locally, but international visitors must verify license requirements, insurance, helmet rules and road conditions before renting. Do not ride on unfamiliar mountain or coastal roads at night.

## Practical Transport Notes

1. **Keep your hotel address in Chinese.** Show it to taxi drivers or present it at station information.
2. **Save offline maps.** Some rural areas have weak mobile signal.
3. **Add buffer time.** Weekday rush hour, festival travel and bad weather can delay trains and buses.
4. **Check luggage rules.** Buses, trains and bike shares have different luggage limits.
5. **Verify entry and license rules.** Scooter and car rental requirements depend on nationality and license type.
