## Guide Positioning

Taiwan is generally convenient for short trips: public transport is easy in major cities, food is widely available, and most tourist signs include English. A smooth trip still depends on preparation around entry requirements, payment, weather, local food habits and transport timing.

This guide is a pre-trip checklist for international visitors. Always confirm visa, health, payment and safety information through official sources close to your travel date.

## Before You Depart

### Passport and Entry Requirements

- Check passport validity for your travel dates and onward departure requirements.
- Confirm visa or visa-exemption rules for your nationality through official immigration sources.
- Keep return or onward travel details, accommodation address and emergency contact information accessible.
- Check whether any health declaration, customs or agricultural-product rules apply.

### SIM Card, eSIM and Wi-Fi

| Option | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Airport SIM card | Visitors who want local data immediately | Check data amount, validity period and whether calls are included. |
| eSIM | Travelers with unlocked phones | Activate before arrival if possible; confirm local network coverage. |
| Portable Wi-Fi | Groups or heavy data users | Compare rental pickup, return, battery and deposit rules. |
| Hotel / cafe Wi-Fi | Light browsing | Do not rely on it for navigation in rural areas. |

### Money and Payment

- **Cash:** Still important for traditional markets, snack stalls, small shops and rural towns.
- **Credit / debit cards:** Widely accepted in hotels, malls, chain restaurants and larger shops.
- **Transport card:** Useful for MRT, buses, YouBike and some convenience stores; check purchase and refund rules.
- **Mobile payment:** Acceptance varies by shop; do not assume every vendor supports the same wallet.
- **ATM:** Check whether your card works locally and notify your bank of travel dates.

### Packing Notes

- Comfortable walking shoes for old streets, night markets and trails.
- Light rain jacket or compact umbrella for sudden showers.
- Sunscreen, hat and water bottle for summer and coastal trips.
- Power adapter and charger; check local voltage before use.
- Reusable bag for snacks, souvenirs and convenience-store purchases.

## Arrival and Transport

### Airport to City

Choose airport MRT, bus, taxi or private transfer based on luggage, arrival time and accommodation. For late-night arrivals, confirm the last train or bus before relying on public transport.

### Getting Around Cities

- **MRT:** Best for central Taipei and major urban districts.
- **Bus:** Covers neighborhoods not reached by rail; verify your stop on a map app.
- **Taxi:** Useful for late nights, luggage and short cross-district trips.
- **YouBike:** Good for short riverside or neighborhood rides; follow parking and helmet rules.
- **Intercity travel:** Compare HSR, TRA train and bus based on destination, luggage and schedule.

### Driving and Scooter Rental

Verify license eligibility, insurance, helmet rules and road conditions before renting a car or scooter. Do not ride unfamiliar coastal or mountain roads at night. If you are not confident on a scooter, choose train, bus, taxi or licensed transfer instead.

## Food and Dining Notes

### Ordering Local Food

- Start with one staple, one side dish and one drink when trying a new market.
- Ask about spice, sweetness and allergens before ordering.
- State dietary restrictions early: no beef, no pork, no seafood, vegetarian or dairy-free.
- Many small stalls prefer cash; seating may be shared.

### Common Dietary Concerns

| Concern | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Vegetarian | Ask whether dishes contain garlic, onion, egg, dairy or meat broth. |
| No beef | Confirm whether noodle soup or broth uses beef stock. |
| Seafood allergy | Ask about oysters, clams, shrimp, fish sauce and shrimp paste. |
| Peanut allergy | Check peanut powder, peanut oil, sesame sauce and dessert toppings. |
| Gluten / wheat sensitivity | Ask about wheat noodles, dumpling wrappers, soy sauce and fried batter. |

### Sweetness and Ice

Taiwanese tea drinks often offer sweetness and ice level choices. If unsure, choose half sugar or less sugar. Hot and cold versions may have different prices.

## Etiquette and Daily Habits

- Queue in order for transport, food and attractions.
- Keep voices low in MRT, temples, cafes and quiet neighborhoods.
- Follow temple rules: dress appropriately, avoid disrupting worshipers and check photography rules.
- Keep night-market walkways clear; do not block stalls with bags or food.
- Use designated trash and recycling areas; do not leave packaging on stalls or streets.
- Tipping is not a standard requirement in most casual restaurants or taxi rides.

## Safety and Practical Information

### Emergency and Help

Save local emergency numbers and your accommodation contact before arrival. For medical help, identify nearby pharmacies, clinics and hospitals in your staying district.

### Weather

- **Summer:** Hot and humid, with afternoon thunderstorms and possible typhoons.
- **Winter:** Cooler in northern Taiwan; mountain and coastal areas can be windy.
- **Coastal trips:** Check tide, wind, surf and beach closure notices.
- **Mountain trips:** Check trail closures, rain and visibility before departure.

### Communication

- English is common in major tourist areas, hotels and chain shops.
- Smaller vendors may use basic English, food names or gestures.
- Save key phrases and your hotel address in Chinese for taxi or emergency use.
- Download offline maps and translation tools before visiting rural areas.

## Common First-Timer Notes

1. **Taiwanese food is not always spicy.** Chili is often added on the side.
2. **Breakfast culture starts early.** Traditional soy-milk shops may sell out popular items before afternoon.
3. **Night markets are dinner plus snacking.** Do not arrive too full to try several small dishes.
4. **Southern and northern flavors differ.** Tainan and other southern cities often favor sweeter, old-style savory dishes.
5. **Shop information changes.** Hours, closed days, prices and menus can update quickly; verify close to your visit.
