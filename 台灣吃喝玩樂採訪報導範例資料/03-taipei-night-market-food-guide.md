## How to Order at a Night Market

1. **Walk the market first.** Observe busy stalls, prices and portions before buying.
2. **Start with two dishes.** Choose one savory staple and one fried snack, then add dessert if you still have room.
3. **Carry cash.** Most small stalls prefer cash; cards and mobile payment are not universal.
4. **Queue in order.** Do not cut in. If the stall has a number system, follow it.
5. **Ask about spice and allergens.** Common allergens include peanut, sesame, seafood, egg, wheat and chili.
6. **Eat near the stall or designated seating.** Do not block the walking lane with bags or food.
7. **Check waste bins.** Markets usually have trash and recycling areas; do not leave packaging on stalls or walkways.
