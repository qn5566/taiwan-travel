## Jiufen and the Mining Hills

### [Jiufen Old Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E4%B9%9D%E4%BB%BD%E8%80%81%E8%A1%97%20%E7%9D%BF%E8%8A%B3%E5%8E%BF)

**Transport:** TRA to Ruifang Station, then bus or taxi up the hill.  
**What to see:** Hillside old street, stone steps, tea houses, souvenir shops, coastal mountain views and evening atmosphere.  
**Suggested stop:** 3–4 hours.  
**Notes:** Streets are narrow and steep; wear comfortable shoes. Check the last bus back to Ruifang before staying for sunset.

### [Jinguashi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E9%87%91%E7%93%9C%E7%9F%B3%20%E7%9D%BF%E8%8A%B3%E5%8E%BF)

**What to see:** Historic gold-mining town, industrial heritage, mountain scenery and nearby mining-heritage sites.  
**Notes:** Combine with Jiufen if you have a full day. Verify museum or site opening hours and closed days.

### Nearby Food and Snacks

Look for taro balls, sweet potato balls, fish balls, peanut ice cream rolls, tea and mountain-style snacks. Prices in tourist old streets can be higher than Taipei local markets; compare portions before buying.

## Practical Notes

1. **Check last transport.** Hill towns and rural stops may have limited evening buses.
2. **Wear walking shoes.** Old streets, waterfalls and coastal rocks involve steps or uneven ground.
3. **Watch weather.** Mountain trails and coastal viewpoints can become slippery or windy after rain.
4. **Bring cash.** Rural old streets and small stalls may not accept cards.
5. **Do not enter restricted areas.** Wetlands, mining sites and cliff edges often have safety boundaries.
