## How to Save Without Choosing Poor Food

1. **Eat where local workers and students eat.** Look for long queues of regulars rather than tourist menus with photos only.
2. **Choose one staple plus one side.** Avoid buying many separate snacks that add up quickly.
3. **Markets and old streets usually offer lower prices** than hotel-adjacent shopping districts.
4. **Check cash price and portion.** Some stalls charge more for extras, toppings or drinks.
5. **Convenience stores work for breakfast, drinks and emergency meals**, but local diners give a stronger travel experience.

## Dietary and Ordering Notes

- **Vegetarian:** Look for tofu, vegetable buns, mushroom noodles, egg dishes and convenience-store vegetarian items.
- **No beef:** Confirm whether soup base contains beef stock before ordering noodle soup.
- **Seafood allergy:** Ask about oysters, clams, fish sauce and shrimp paste.
- **Peanut allergy:** Many snacks and desserts use peanut powder or peanut oil.
- **Spice:** Chili sauce is often served on the side; ask for it separately if unsure.
